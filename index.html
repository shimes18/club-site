What is Hack Club?

Hack club is a community for teenager to make projects , doesnt matter whether you're just starting out or seasoned proffessional.

> Hack club provides you the resources to make the project you have been dreaming of making.
> We also provide proper enviornment and guidance to make projects.
> It is an evergrowing community of teenagers and a safe space for every member.
